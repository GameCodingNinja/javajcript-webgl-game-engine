In addition to writing this game, this is also my game engine. I've written many game engines over the years (first one being a software rendering engine before video cards) and many years ago got the the idea to rewrite my Vulkan game engine written in C++ to pure JavaScript do to the game support in HTML (webGL, Web Audio API, Gamepad API, etc).

Game supports, mouse / keyboard, gamePad, mobile and full screen. Has key binding feature.

Description:
Aliens are attacking and destroying the city. You are the only one left to keep the enemies at bay.

Controls:
Default controls (can be changed in Key Bindings menu)
Keyboard: W: up, S: down. A: left, S: right, SPACE: shoot, LEFT SHIFT: Boost, E: shields, Esc: Pause game
Game-pad: Movement: D-Pad, Left Stick, A: Shoot, B: Boost, Y: Shields, Start: Pause game
Mobile: Left side of screen acts as Left Stick. Center point is where it is touched. Right side of screen, tap to shoot, swipe up and hold to boost, two finger touch for shields. Hold down 1 finger for rapid fire when feature is available. Swipe across the screen from right to left to pause the game
