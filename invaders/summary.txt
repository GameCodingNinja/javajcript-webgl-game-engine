========================================================================
  BOSS00 - PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY
========================================================================

WHAT IT IS
----------
boss00 is a big new boss enemy for the Invaders game. It flies in,
picks a building far from the player, and tries to destroy it with a
beam. The player has to chase the boss down and shoot it enough to
interrupt the attack. Do that four times and the boss dies.

Because "a boss is just an enemy," boss00 lives inside the normal
enemy system (the "_enemy_" strategy) alongside enemy00/01/02.


HOW THE FIGHT WORKS
-------------------
1. The boss descends onto a building that is FAR from the player
   (so the player has to fly across the map to reach it).

2. Once over the building it starts the ATTACK:
     - A beam appears under the boss and expands to full width (~1s).
     - After the beam is full, a 5-second countdown begins.
     - 1 second in, the building starts SHAKING - slowly at first,
       then faster and harder (it only shakes downward, never up).
     - If the countdown finishes, the building is destroyed (it falls
       like any other destroyed building) and the beam retracts.

3. While the boss is attacking it is VULNERABLE - the player can shoot
   it. Two bars appear above the boss (both start full and green):
     - HIT BAR  - drains one notch per hit. This is the "interrupt" meter.
     - HEALTH BAR - the boss's life.

4. Two ways the attack can end:
     - PLAYER WINS THE EXCHANGE (empties the hit bar before the 5s):
       INTERRUPT! The boss stops, loses 1 HEALTH, pauses ~1 second so
       you can see the change, then races off to the next building.
     - BOSS WINS THE EXCHANGE (5s runs out first):
       The building is destroyed, the boss loses NO health, and it
       races off to the next building.

5. While flying between buildings the boss is NOT shootable, but it
   still moves fast and can crash into the player for damage.

6. Repeat until the HEALTH bar is empty -> the boss dies, spinning and
   falling quickly off the bottom of the screen.

Bars are only shown while the boss can be hit (during an attack).


TUNING (all in source/state/gamedefs.js)
-----------------------------------------
BOSS00_HEALTH_MAX .......... how many interrupts kill the boss
BOSS00_HIT_BAR_MAX ......... hits needed to interrupt one attack
BOSS00_DESTROY_TIME ........ how long the boss needs to destroy a building
BOSS00_BEAM_WIDTH .......... beam full width in pixels
BOSS00_BEAM_GROW_SEC ....... beam expand time
BOSS00_BEAM_RETRACT_SEC .... beam retract time
BOSS00_SHAKE_DELAY ......... wait before the building starts shaking
BOSS00_SHAKE_*_START/END ... shake speed + strength ramp
BOSS00_MOVE_PIXELS_PER_SEC . travel speed to the next building
BOSS00_DESCEND_PIXELS_PER_SEC  first descent speed
BOSS00_RESTING_Y ........... attack height above the buildings


WHAT WAS BUILT (BY PHASE)
-------------------------
Phase 1 - Data / art / strategy
  * boss00_ship node added to enemy.strategy: a NULL head sprite that
    carries the collision, with children for the beam, the visible
    body, and the two progress bars.
  * Art (null head, body, beam, health bar, hit bar) added to the
    SOURCE list (level1DataList.lst) and the sprite sheet regenerated.

Phase 2 - AI (source/scripts/boss00aiscripts.js + data/objects/ai/boss00.ai)
  * Behavior tree: Descend -> DestroyBuilding -> repeat(Pick+Move ->
    DestroyBuilding).
  * Picks a non-destroyed, not-already-targeted building far from the
    player; flies there fast (wrap-around aware).

Phase 3 - Beam + shake visuals
  * Beam shows, widens to full, then narrows/retracts when done or
    interrupted.
  * Building shakes progressively faster during the destroy countdown.

Phase 4 - Damage model (source/scripts/boss00scripts.js + level1state.js)
  * Player shots drain the hit bar (only while vulnerable).
  * Empty hit bar -> interrupt -> lose 1 health -> flee.
  * Empty health bar -> boss dies (Boss00Ship_Die).
  * Boss can damage the player by colliding with it.
  * Bars turned green.

Phase 5 - Verify
  * Builds clean; no per-frame memory allocations; movement is
    frame-rate independent; state resets correctly on restart.


IMPORTANT GOTCHA (for future enemies with children)
---------------------------------------------------
A strategy node WITH child nodes becomes a "SpriteNode", and the engine
does NOT auto-prepare the head sprite's own prepareOnInit scripts (like
the AI). So after creating such a boss you must call
    node.get().prepareScriptOnInit()
or its AI silently never runs. (Simple childless enemies don't need this.)
See AGENTS.md -> "Adding a New Enemy or Boss".
